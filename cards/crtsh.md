# Source Card: crt.sh

- **Type:** certificate-transparency search service
- **Inputs:** domain, certificate
- **Outputs:** certificate records and candidate domain relationships
- **URL:** https://crt.sh/
- **Stage:** collect

## Useful for

Finding certificate-transparency observations related to domains and subdomains.

## Limitations

A certificate record is historical technical evidence. It does not prove current DNS state, active service or organizational ownership.

## Verify with

DNS history, RDAP, certificate details and other infrastructure sources.
