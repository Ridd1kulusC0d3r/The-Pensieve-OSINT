# Source Card: VirusTotal

- **Type:** multi-source defensive enrichment service
- **Inputs:** URL, domain, IP, hash
- **Outputs:** reputation/enrichment context
- **URL:** https://www.virustotal.com/
- **Stage:** collect / verify

## Useful for

Cross-vendor context around defensive observables.

## Limitations

Vendor/community labels may conflict, lag or reflect different detection criteria. One detection does not prove malicious intent or actor attribution.

## Verify with

Underlying vendor reports, original samples/URLs where safely available, infrastructure context and independent threat-intelligence sources.
